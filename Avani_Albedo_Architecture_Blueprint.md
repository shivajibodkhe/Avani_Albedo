**Team Name:** Avani Albedo  
**Slogan:** Optimizing Albedo, Cooling the Earth.

---

# Executive Summary

Avani Albedo is an agentic urban-climate decision-support platform that combines Google Earth Engine, BigQuery Geospatial Analytics, Vertex AI Gemini, Cloud Run, App Engine, and Google Maps Platform to transform urban heat observations into explainable, budget-aware climate interventions.

Unlike traditional GIS systems that stop at identifying heat hotspots, Avani Albedo recommends actionable interventions such as:

- Tree canopy expansion
- Cool roof deployment
- Shade structures
- Hydration stations

The platform evaluates cost, feasibility, population impact, and projected cooling benefits before generating an optimized climate action plan.

# End-to-End Workflow

## Step 1: User Selects Area

Planner selects:

- City
- Neighborhood
- Custom polygon

Example:

```json
{
  "city": "Bengaluru",
  "budget": 10000000,
  "objective": "maximize_public_health"
}
```

---

## Step 2: Earth Engine Analysis

Compute:

- Land Surface Temperature (LST)
- NDVI
- NDBI
- Albedo
- Heat anomalies

Data Sources:

- Landsat 8/9
- Sentinel
- OpenStreetMap
- Public infrastructure datasets

Output:

```json
{
  "cell_id":"BLR_001",
  "lst":41.2,
  "ndvi":0.14,
  "albedo":0.18
}
```

---

## Step 3: BigQuery Spatial Intelligence

Store climate cells and infrastructure.

Datasets:

```text
avani_raw
avani_climate
avani_city
avani_intelligence
avani_scenarios
avani_audit
```

Spatial joins identify:

- Schools
- Hospitals
- Transit stops
- Population exposure

---

## Step 4: Heat Vulnerability Index

Formula:

```text
HVI =
0.30 Heat Severity
+0.20 Vegetation Deficit
+0.15 Population Exposure
+0.15 Critical Infrastructure Exposure
+0.10 Built Density
+0.10 Historical Heat Persistence
```

Output:

```text
0-30 Low
30-50 Moderate
50-70 High
70-85 Severe
85-100 Critical
```

---
