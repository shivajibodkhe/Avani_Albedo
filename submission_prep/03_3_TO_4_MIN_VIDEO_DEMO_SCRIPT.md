## 🏆 Top Recording Tips for a Perfect 3:00 Flat Video

1. **Pre-test the timing:** Read the script along with your clicks once or twice. Aim for **2:55 to 2:59** to never cross the 3:00 mark.
2. **Resolution:** Record in **1080p Full HD (1920x1080)** with your browser zoomed to 100%.
3. **No interruptions:** Keep tabs already opened and credentials saved so login takes literally 2 seconds.
4. **Google Drive / YouTube Link:** When uploading, set visibility to **"Anyone with the link can view"** (or YouTube Unlisted).
