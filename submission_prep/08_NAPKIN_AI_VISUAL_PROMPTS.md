## 📌 Prompt 3: 4 Core Interventions & Physical Land Feasibility (For Slide 5 & 7)

> **How to use in Napkin AI:**  
> Paste the text below into Napkin AI, select all, and click **"Generate Visual"**. Select the **"Grid / Feature Matrix"** style.

```text
The 4 Prescriptive Cooling Interventions & Physical Feasibility Matrix

1. Urban Tree Canopy & Native Shading:
• Cooling Impact: -2.0°C to -3.5°C localized temperature drop via evapotranspiration.
• Soil Requirement: Strictly requires at least 16 m² (4m × 4m) of unsealed, permeable ground soil per tree.
• Strategic Role: Long-term ecological resilience and particulate air pollution (PM2.5) filtration.
• Deployment Window: Monsoon planting season (July to September).

• Cooling Impact: -1.8°C to -2.5°C surface drop by reflecting 88% of incoming solar radiation.
• Soil Requirement: Exactly 0 m² of ground soil required!
• Strategic Role: Immediate rapid deployment on commercial, industrial, and public school rooftops in concrete-dense wards.
• Cost Efficiency: Highest cooling degree reduction per rupee invested.

3. Tensile Solar Shade Canopies:
• Cooling Impact: -4.0°C to -6.5°C perceived thermal comfort improvement under shade.
• Soil Requirement: Minimal anchor footprint along sidewalks, bus stops, and open-air markets.
• Strategic Role: Rapid pedestrian heat-stroke relief along high-traffic transit corridors.

4. Smart Public Hydration Kiosks:
• Health Impact: Direct mitigation of acute dehydration, heat exhaustion, and hospital heat admissions.
• Infrastructure: Connected to clean municipal water pipelines with chilled dispensing points.
• Strategic Role: Deployed within 300 meters of outdoor worker congregating points and hospitals.
```
