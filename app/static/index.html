🔬 Scientific Satellite Data Lineage & Calibration Standards

Multi-sensor earth observation telemetry • Peer-reviewed radiative transfer formulas • Ground-truth AWS validation

🛰️ NASA / USGS Landsat 8 & 9 TIRS

Thermal Infrared Sensor (TIRS-2) Band 10 (10.60–11.19 µm) at 100m raw resolution (resampled to 30m). Measures Top-of-Atmosphere (TOA) spectral radiance.

LST = T_b / (1 + (λ · T_b / ρ) · ln(ε))
🛰️ ESA Copernicus Sentinel-2 MSI

Multi-Spectral Instrument at 10m spatial resolution. Band 4 (Red, 665nm) and Band 8 (NIR, 842nm) compute vegetation vigor and impervious built surface indices.

NDVI = (B8 - B4) / (B8 + B4) • NDBI = (B11 - B8)/(B11 + B8)

📐 Thermodynamic Calibration & Albedo Inversion

1. Planck Radiance Inversion:
Inverts spectral radiance \(L_\lambda\) to Brightness Temperature using calibration constants \(K_1 = 774.8853\), \(K_2 = 1321.0789\):
T_sensor = K_2 / ln((K_1 / L_λ) + 1)
2. Liang (2001) Surface Broadband Albedo:
Shortwave broadband albedo computed across multi-spectral reflectance bands:
α = 0.356ρ₁ + 0.130ρ₃ + 0.373ρ₄ + 0.085ρ₅ + 0.072ρ₇ - 0.0018

⚡ Complete 8-Technology Google Cloud Coverage

🤖 1. Gemini on Vertex AI

Role: Prescriptive reasoning & executive briefing.
Generates persona-specific action directives (Municipal Commissioner, Public Health, Urban Planner) with thermodynamic justifications.

📊 2. BigQuery (Spatial SQL)

Role: Analyzing planetary environmental & demographic datasets.
Executes ST_GEOGFROMTEXT and ST_INTERSECTS on millions of satellite pixels and ward census polygons.

🧠 3. Vertex AI (Predictive Models)

Role: Multi-day heat persistence & UHI forecasting.
Predicts 24-hour diurnal thermal flux, peak solar insolation, and hazard duration exceeding 38°C.

🌍 4. Google Earth Engine (GEE)

Role: Planetary-scale multi-spectral image processing.
Ingests raw Landsat 8/9 TIRS and Sentinel-2 MSI granules to compute real-time LST, NDVI, and NDBI layers.

📦 5. Cloud Storage (GCS)

Role: Staging geo-spatial raster imagery & vector polygons.
Stores multi-temporal GeoTIFF tiles, boundary GeoJSONs, and historical decadal timelapse layers.

👁️ 6. Vision AI (Image Analysis)

Role: Aerial & drone roof classification.
Analyzes high-res satellite imagery to categorize rooftop material types (asphalt, concrete, corrugated metal) and estimate SRI retrofit potential.

🚀 7. Cloud Run (Serverless Deployment)

Role: High-performance containerized resilience engine.
Runs the FastAPI application and Google OR-Tools Mixed-Integer Linear Programming solver with auto-scaling to zero.

📢 8. Pub/Sub (Event-Driven Alerts)

Role: Real-time heatwave dispatch pipelines.
Dispatches automated webhook alerts to municipal emergency teams, mobile drinking water tankers, and hospital syndromic systems.
