🔬 Scientific Satellite Data Lineage & Calibration Standards

Multi-sensor earth observation telemetry • Peer-reviewed radiative transfer formulas • Ground-truth AWS validation

🛰️ NASA / USGS Landsat 8 & 9 TIRS

Thermal Infrared Sensor (TIRS-2) Band 10 (10.60–11.19 µm) at 100m raw resolution (resampled to 30m). Measures Top-of-Atmosphere (TOA) spectral radiance.

LST = T_b / (1 + (λ · T_b / ρ) · ln(ε))
🛰️ ESA Copernicus Sentinel-2 MSI

Multi-Spectral Instrument at 10m spatial resolution. Band 4 (Red, 665nm) and Band 8 (NIR, 842nm) compute vegetation vigor and impervious built surface indices.

NDVI = (B8 - B4) / (B8 + B4) • NDBI = (B11 - B8)/(B11 + B8)

📐 Thermodynamic Calibration & Albedo Inversion

1. Planck Radiance Inversion:
Inverts spectral radiance \(L_\lambda\) to Brightness Temperature using calibration constants \(K_1 = 774.8853\), \(K_2 = 1321.0789\):
T_sensor = K_2 / ln((K_1 / L_λ) + 1)
2. Liang (2001) Surface Broadband Albedo:
Shortwave broadband albedo computed across multi-spectral reflectance bands:
α = 0.356ρ₁ + 0.130ρ₃ + 0.373ρ₄ + 0.085ρ₅ + 0.072ρ₇ - 0.0018
